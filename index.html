You might recognize this.

If three or more of these are true, the rest of this page is for you.

  1. Your business stalls when you take a week off.
  2. You've watched the top people in your field and tried to adapt how they work. It never quite fits.
  3. You have a methodology in your head that's never been written down.
  4. New hires can't replicate what you do, even when you explain it.
  5. You're trading time for money because there's no leveraged version of your work.
  6. You've outgrown the version of your business that runs on memory.

The work.

Not a template. Not someone else's framework. The one that's actually yours.

I'm Kimberly. My background is instructional design — the discipline of getting what someone knows out of their head and into a form other people can use. I now apply that to small businesses.

I work with expert practitioners — coaches, realtors, photographers, childcare directors, consultants — whose businesses run on their expertise, and only on their expertise. We work together for a quarter. I sit with you, extract how your work actually works, and build the one thing your business needs to keep running when you're not in the room.

One finished thing, in a quarter.

What we build depends on where your business needs the leverage. Three examples of what a quarter has looked like:

  • For a coach.

    Twelve years of methodology, extracted from how she actually teaches and structured into a curriculum she can publish as written content now and assemble into a paid course later. One effort, two products.

  • For a realtor.

    The ten-week post-sale follow-up sequence she'd been hand-cranking from memory, built into a sequence she runs without thinking. Same warmth, none of the cognitive load.

  • For a studio owner.

    A content system that turns the work she's already doing — sessions, client conversations, behind-the-scenes — into a steady publication rhythm that runs without becoming a second job.

If someone else's system was going to work for you, it would have by now.

The engagement.

One quarter. We start with a scoping conversation, then move into weekly working sessions where I extract and you refine. By the end of the quarter, you walk away with one finished, working thing — built, not just planned.

Quarterly engagement, invoiced at the start. A one-page scope of work before we begin. Plain pricing, sent in advance of any decision.

A good fit.

This works well for

Experts whose businesses are real, working, and bottlenecked at their own time. People who know their craft cold and are ready to build the system underneath it.

This isn't the right fit for

Startups still figuring out what they're building. People looking for a VA to execute tasks they've already mapped. People making decisions from cold ads or cold outreach.

A first conversation.

Thirty minutes. No pitch, no slides. We'll spend it on where your business actually bottlenecks. If we're a fit, we'll talk about next steps. If we're not, you'll still walk away with a clearer read on where you're stuck.
