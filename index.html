The work.

Not a template. Not someone else's framework. The one that's actually yours.

Why I don't bring a framework.

Most small business owners looking for answers turn to others in their field — watching what the top people do, trying to adapt their systems. It rarely works, because your business is uniquely yours.

I don't arrive with a framework and fit you to it. I extract what you already know and build it into something that runs the way you actually work.

If someone else's system was going to work for you, it would have by now.

The engagement.

One quarter. We start with a scoping conversation, then move into weekly working sessions where I extract and you refine. By the end of the quarter, you walk away with one finished, working thing — built, not just planned.

Quarterly engagement, invoiced at the start. A one-page scope of work before we begin. Plain pricing, sent in advance of any decision.

A good fit.

This works well for: Experts whose businesses are real, working, and bottlenecked at their own time. People who know their craft cold and are ready to build the system underneath it.

This isn't the right fit for: Startups still figuring out what they're building. People looking for a VA to execute tasks they've already mapped. People making decisions from cold ads or cold outreach.

A first conversation.

Thirty minutes. No pitch, no slides. We'll spend it on where your business actually bottlenecks. If we're a fit, we'll talk about next steps. If we're not, you'll still walk away with a clearer read on where you're stuck.
